Privacy Policy for OnDock

Last Updated: October 23, 2025

Introduction

OnDock ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile information you provide

2. Location Data

We collect and use your device's location data to:

  • Show nearby docks and waypoints
  • Provide navigation and routing services
  • Share your location with other users when using matching features
  • Display location-based content and recommendations

You can disable location access in your device settings, but this may limit app functionality.

3. Camera and Media

We request camera permissions to:

  • Take photos for your profile
  • Capture images for posts and content sharing
  • Scan QR codes for dock check-ins

We only access your camera when you explicitly choose to use these features.

4. Microphone/Audio

We may request microphone access for:

  • Voice messages or audio recordings
  • Video recording with audio

We only access your microphone when you explicitly use audio/video features.

5. Device Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version
  • IP address

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process your registration and authentication
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send you notifications (with your consent)
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We Share Your Information With:

  • Other Users: When you use social features
  • Service Providers: Firebase, Google Maps, Push notifications
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law

We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with advertisers without consent
  • Use your camera/microphone without your explicit action

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure password hashing
  • Encrypted data storage
  • Regular security audits

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of location services or notifications
  • Request your data in a portable format
